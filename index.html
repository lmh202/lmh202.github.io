Research Experience

Radar + Camera BEV Fusion (DifFUSER-based)
Jul 2025 – Oct 2025
  • Built a BEV fusion pipeline using millimeter-wave radar point clouds and cameras as a low-cost alternative to LiDAR.
  • Integrated a weather classifier with WRGNet to adapt fusion weights across weather conditions for improved robustness.
  • Achieved 0.52 mIoU on the target setting, demonstrating competitive performance for a low-cost radar-camera alternative to LiDAR.
Inverse Rendering for Autonomous Driving
Ongoing
  • Exploring inverse rendering pipelines for autonomous-driving scenes with a focus on lighting, materials, and geometry-aware scene understanding.
  • Investigating how multi-view reconstruction and scene representations can improve downstream perception and world modeling.
